==============================================================================
ROBUSTNESS -- dropping 2009 and 2020 (the GFC and COVID recessions)
==============================================================================
MOTIVATION (trap #7). In 2009 and 2020 the deficit exploded AND private
investment collapsed -- but both were caused by the recession, not by each
other. Automatic stabilisers widen the deficit in a downturn by construction.
So a negative deficit coefficient in a sample containing those years may be
measuring reverse causation (recession -> deficit) rather than the
confidence channel GEAR posited (deficit -> lost confidence -> investment).
These two years are 2 of 31 observations but are by far the largest outliers
in both variables. Dropping them is a diagnostic, not a preferred estimate.
